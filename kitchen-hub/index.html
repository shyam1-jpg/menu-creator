New: food-law labels and diet checks, built in

The two jobs other kitchen tools leave to a Word document and a guess.

🏷️

Natasha's Law label in one click

Pick a recipe and get a print-ready PPDS label PDF: the food name, full ingredients in weight order with the 14 allergens in bold, use-by date, storage advice and your business name and address. Comes as a 50×80 mm label or an A4 sheet of 12, made from the recipe, so nothing is copied out by hand.
✅

Dietary rules check

Check a dish or a whole menu against Vegetarian, Vegan, No onion/garlic, Jain, Halal and Egg-free. You get a pass or fail for each one, with the ingredients that broke the rule: onion family, root vegetables for Jain, or gelatine and alcohol for Halal. It even notes when asafoetida (hing) may contain wheat.

You remain responsible for checking supplier ingredient information. The Hub makes the check fast and consistent.

Questions

Do I need special software?Microsoft Excel for Windows (desktop). Macros must be enabled.
Where is my data stored?In the workbook on your own computer. Nothing is uploaded unless you choose to use the AI helper.
Does it meet Natasha's Law (PPDS labelling)?Yes, the Natasha's Law Label button prints labels with the name, full ingredients with the 14 allergens in bold, use-by date, storage advice, and your business name and address. You still need to check that your supplier ingredient information is right.
Can it check Jain, Halal or no onion/garlic menus?Yes, the Dietary Rules Check tests any recipe or the whole menu against Vegetarian, Vegan, No onion/garlic, Jain, Halal and Egg-free, and lists the ingredients that fail. You can edit the word lists yourself.
Does it handle the 14 UK allergens?Yes — every recipe tracks the 14 allergens and labels show them clearly. You remain responsible for checking information is correct.
